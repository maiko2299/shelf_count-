Shelf Count

Say or type what's on the rack. The app finds the closest part on the ManagerPlus list, even from half a part number or a description. Counters never see system quantities.

How to talk to it

  1. Announce the shelf: shelf A, now on shelf dee, or rack CI2 shelf A.
  2. Say part, then count: EC350 one, HP kit three.
  3. No clear number? Use the description: Lochinvar touch screen 1, PN guess 1100317.
  4. Say next between parts, or tap Add. Several parts can go in one breath.
  5. Nothing there: Crosby PRV 100 psi not found logs a zero.
  6. Yellow cards need one tap. Red cards aren't on this rack's list: the app checks the rest of the warehouse, or you log the part as "not in system" with a photo.
